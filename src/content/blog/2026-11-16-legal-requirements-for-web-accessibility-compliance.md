## Accessibility - are you meeting legal requirements?

Essential guidelines exist to allow web accessibility professionals to ensure their websites follow consistent best practices.

These guidelines work alongside legal requirements that ensure accessibility is a fundamental human right.

These laws fit into three types of category:

1. Civil Rights Law. These laws aim to prevent discrimination against people with disabilities.
2. Procurement Law. These laws ensure that only products or services that meet accessibility standards can be purchased or acquired.
3. Industry Specific Law. These laws ensure accessibility is considered within specific industries.
